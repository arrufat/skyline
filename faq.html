Skyline questions

Why doesn't a two-hour row's rain match the two hours added up?

Skyline adds the exact amounts, then rounds. 2.76 mm and 5.07 mm show as 2.8 and 5.1 by the hour, but together they make 7.83, so the two-hour row shows 7.8.

Why is the chance of rain so high sometimes?

It's Open-Meteo's chance of more than 0.1 mm in that hour, anywhere in a square about 27 km wide. A few drops nearby count, so check the amount next to the hour. A day's chance is its wettest hour.

Why does Skyline's rain differ from other apps in Spain?

In Spain, Skyline averages Open-Meteo's rain with Météo-France's AROME for the next two days. Checked against rain gauges for a year, the average was right more often than either one.

Why do I see the next four hours' rain only sometimes?

The card needs a forecast with rain every 15 minutes, which only parts of Europe and North America have, and it only shows when rain is coming.

Is the rain map radar?

It's the forecast, from two hours ago to six hours ahead. It's smoother than radar and can miss a small shower.

Does Skyline track me?

Your places stay on your phone, and Skyline has no server. Your phone asks the weather services directly. The privacy policy lists them.

Why isn't my widget up to date?

It updates about once an hour. If it falls behind, set Skyline's battery use to unrestricted in your phone's settings.

Why are there no warnings where I live?

Skyline shows each country's official warnings, but a few countries, such as Australia or Mexico, don't publish them in a way it can read yet.

Where are the other tabs?

Basic shows three tabs. Switch to Advanced in Settings, under Forecast, to see every tab and reorder them.
